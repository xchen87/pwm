# Personal World Model

## One-line thesis
A user-controlled model of your life that remembers what matters, understands what changed, and lets specialized AI agents act with your permission.

## Core idea
**One world model. Infinite personal agents.**

The world model represents:
- people and relationships
- things and ownership
- commitments
- decisions and rationale
- priorities
- events
- documents
- temporal history
- provenance and confidence

Agents are applications on top of this substrate:
- Life Radar
- Customer Advocate
- Ownership
- Relationship
- Decision / Memory
- Family
- Financial/Admin

## MVP
Connect Gmail + Google Calendar, add explicit memories, and receive:
1. Your World
2. World Brief
3. Ask Your World
4. Memory
5. Commitment Radar
6. Decision Memory
7. Source inspection
8. Customer Advocate drafts

## Product promise
> Connect your life. We'll remember what matters, tell you what changed, and catch things before they become problems.

## Repository structure

- `CLAUDE.md` — persistent instructions for Claude Code
- `AGENT.md` — step-by-step autonomous implementation plan
- `TECHNICAL_BRIEF.md` — product and architecture specification
- `investor_pitch.pptx` — investor/co-founder pitch
- `docs/` — supporting product and research docs
- `prompts/` — agent/evaluation prompts

## Build philosophy
The moat is not the foundation model. It is the persistent, temporal, user-controlled world model, plus provenance, permissions, trust, and action history.
